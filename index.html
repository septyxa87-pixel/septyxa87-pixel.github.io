ACTON Studio

The publishing assistant of ACTON, a French-language documentary channel about true stories, disasters, mysteries, space and civilisations — youtube.com/@ACTON87.

What ACTON Studio is

ACTON Studio is a desktop application used by the creator of the ACTON channel to produce and publish ACTON's own documentary videos: three short videos (30–60 seconds) and one 3–5 minute video per day. It runs on the creator's own computer. This page is public and requires no sign-in; the application itself has no web interface.

What it does

  1. Finds topics. Looks at public statistics of French-language history videos and at Wikipedia page views to choose historical events that French-speaking viewers are interested in.
  2. Writes and checks scripts. Writes an original French script and checks every date and figure against several independent sources.
  3. Produces the video. Synthetic narration (disclosed to viewers), freely licensed archive images with authors credited, editing, French subtitles and automatic quality checks.
  4. Publishes on ACTON. Uploads the finished video to the ACTON channel with its title, description, playlist, subtitles and thumbnail, schedules the publication time, and then checks that every setting was applied.
  5. Learns from results. Reads ACTON's own statistics (views, watch time, retention, subscribers) to choose better topics and formats.

Why it asks for access to a Google account

To publish and measure ACTON's videos, the channel owner signs in once with Google and allows:

PermissionUsed for
Upload YouTube videos (youtube.upload)uploading ACTON's finished videos
Manage YouTube videos (youtube.force-ssl)playlists, French subtitles, thumbnails, publication time and disclosures of ACTON's own videos
View YouTube account (youtube.readonly)reading ACTON's channel and video details to verify uploads
View YouTube Analytics reports (yt-analytics.readonly)measuring the performance of ACTON's videos

ACTON Studio does not access e-mail, contacts, Drive or any other Google data, and it does not access other people's accounts. How data is used, stored and deleted: Privacy Policy.
